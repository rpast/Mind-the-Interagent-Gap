SOURCES USED
Mind the Interagent Gap

Every source below either backs a row in data/ or changed one during the
2026-10-26 verification pass. This file is the audit trail: per-row
provenance, corrections, and what was screened and produced nothing.

Leads that back nothing, access caveats, and candidate additions belong in
RESOURCES.md, not here. A source may appear in both files; its note belongs
to exactly one of them.
