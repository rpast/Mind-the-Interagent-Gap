## Context

**Setting.** Real US futures and equity markets with real losses on May 6, 2010. Quote: “On May 6, 2010, the prices of many U.S.-based equity products experienced an extraordinarily rapid decline and recovery.”

## What happened

A modified algorithm was able to cause dramatic price volatility and disrupted trading in the US stock exchange.

## Mechanism

**Mechanism.** The Sell Algorithm fed orders; HFTs absorbed then resold them; volume rose and the algorithm sped up; prices fell and liquidity vanished; a CME pause stopped the E-Mini fall; liquidity providers' systems paused and stub quotes executed at extreme prices. Quote: “The combined selling pressure from the Sell Algorithm, HFTs and other traders drove the price of the E-Mini down approximately 3% in just four minutes”

## Agents and interaction

**Agents.** A large trader's automated Sell Algorithm, high-frequency traders, cross-market arbitrageurs and the automated trading systems of many liquidity providers; the report counts firms and systems, not agents. Quote: “automated trading systems used by many liquidity providers temporarily paused in reaction to the sudden price declines”

**Interaction.** HFTs bought the Sell Algorithm's orders and resold them, trading among themselves; the Sell Algorithm responded to the resulting volume by selling faster. Automated systems paused in reaction to price moves. Quote: “The Sell Algorithm used by the large trader responded to the increased volume by increasing the rate at which it was feeding the orders into the market”

**Cooperation.** The systems were counterparties trading against each other, not working together; nothing was coordinated or intended as joint action. Quote: “HFTs began to quickly buy and then resell contracts to each other”

**Multiplicity.** The report does not describe an attack.

**Objectives.** The Sell Algorithm aimed to sell 75,000 contracts at a volume-based rate; HFTs aimed to keep small inventories; arbitrageurs to profit from price gaps. Aims were separate and partly opposed. Quote: “target an execution rate set to 9% of the trading volume calculated over the previous minute, but without regard to price or time”
