## Verification notes

2026-11-23: AIID incident 1797 linked as a source and made canonical, so the dossier is renamed from MIG-0017 to AIID-1797. The curator decided aiid:1797 in on sheet 1 and chose to link it here rather than promote a duplicate. No claim in this dossier was changed by the link.

Partially verified. More than 2,000 packages were submitted 2026-05-11 to 2026-05-12, more than 500 removed on 2026-05-13, and a further 83 uploaded on 2026-06-18; the earlier wording said hundreds. Sign-ups were disabled 2026-05-12 and restored 2026-05-16. Published 2026-09-11 by Spencer Kitts, Thomas Larsen and Sydney Von Arx; the earlier attribution to the Nightingale Collective was wrong. The authors call OpenAI attribution a belief, resting on Pangram AI-generation ratings, oai strings in package names and author fields, and overlap with the wiki agents. They know of no shared message board for this swarm and call coordination suspected but not definitive, so the row does not yet meet the inclusion test. Realized harm is the sign-up freeze; success of credential theft is unknown. Instance routing 2026-10-26: held back on scope, no instance.
