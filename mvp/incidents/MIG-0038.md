## Context

**Setting.** Real-world posts circulated on social platforms in early 2025 and were analysed by ISD. Quote: “at least 135 discrete posts analyzed by ISD”

## What happened

Researchers at the Institute for Strategic Dialogue (ISD) report that Operation Overload (also known as Matryoshka or Storm-1679) is a Russian-aligned campaign leveraging purported AI-generated voiceovers and visual impersonations to spread false or inflammatory content across platforms. The campaign reportedly involved at least 135 discrete posts analyzed by ISD in early 2025 targeting institutions and individuals, including one purported viral video claiming USAID funded celebrity trips to Ukraine (see Incident 1061).

## Mechanism

**Mechanism.** The operation spread false content using AI-generated voiceovers and visual impersonations, with posts then amplified by bots; no step-by-step account is given. Quote: “leveraging purported AI-generated voiceovers and visual impersonations”

## Agents and interaction

**Agents.** The page names deepfake and synthetic audio generation tools used by a Russian-aligned operation, and a Washington Post excerpt says a network of bots, of unstated size, amplified the posts. Quote: “liked and reshared mostly by a network of bots”

**Interaction.** The page shows bots liking and resharing the operation's posts, but does not say the posts were made by an AI agent, so no agent is shown acting on another agent's output.

**Cooperation.** The page describes the activity as coordinated by the campaign, which implies intended joint action, but does not describe how the bots and the content production worked together. Quote: “coordinated AI-generated disinformation activity”

**Multiplicity.** The bot network supplied most of the engagement the posts received; the page says nothing more about what being many contributed. Quote: “liked and reshared mostly by a network of bots”

**Objectives.** The operation sought to shift public opinion on the war in Ukraine by impersonating media, academics and law enforcement. Quote: “aims to shift public opinion on the war in Ukraine”
