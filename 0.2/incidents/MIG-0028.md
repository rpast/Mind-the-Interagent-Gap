## Context

**Setting.** Live Wikipedia pages edited by bots deployed on the real site, with real effects on public articles; the bots' operators ran them. Quote: “bot-on-bot editing wars raging on Wikipedia's pages”

## What happened

Wikipedia bots meant to remove vandalism clash with each other and form feedback loops of repetitve undoing of the other bot's edits.

## Mechanism

**Mechanism.** The bots repeatedly undid each other's edits in feedback loops; one report says this went on for over a decade. Quote: “have been waging a cyber-war between each other for over a decade”

## Agents and interaction

**Agents.** Wikipedia editing bots meant to remove vandalism, at least two, unnamed and uncounted. Quote: “Wikipedia bots meant to remove vandalism clash with each other”

**Interaction.** Each bot undoes the other's edits, so each acts on the other's output. Quote: “form feedback loops of repetitve undoing”

**Cooperation.** The bots worked against each other rather than together; the page does not say whether cooperation was intended. Quote: “clash with each other”

**Multiplicity.** Not an attack.

**Objectives.** Each bot was meant to remove vandalism: the same aim. Quote: “Wikipedia bots meant to remove vandalism”
