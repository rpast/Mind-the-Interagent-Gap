## Context

**Setting.** A real UK government public consultation in 2023. Quote: “AI bots flooded a government consultation with tens of thousands of fake responses”

## What happened

Purported AI bots reportedly submitted 90,835 fraudulent responses to a UK government consultation on tobacco and vaping, mostly opposing disposable-vape restrictions. The submissions made up 76% of responses and distorted the apparent balance of public opinion. The Department of Health and Social Care excluded them from its analysis. The operators and underlying AI technology were not identified.

## Mechanism
