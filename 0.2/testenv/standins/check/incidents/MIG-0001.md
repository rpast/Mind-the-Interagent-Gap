## Context

Made up for the test run. Example Logistics and its supplier are fictional.

## What happened

A purchasing bot at Example Logistics raised its standing order each time the supplier's
pricing bot raised its price, and the pricing bot raised the price each time orders rose.
Over two days the order book grew twentyfold before a person stopped it.

## Mechanism

A feedback loop between two bots' rules: higher orders raised the price, and a higher price
raised orders.

## Agents and interaction

Two agents run by two companies. Each acted on the other's output.

## Response

The orders were cancelled by hand and both bots were paused.
